About the Event

What is Xavathon?

Xavathon is Kolkata's most vibrant college-organised marathon, hosted by St. Xavier's College, Kolkata - one of India's premier institutions with a 160-year legacy of excellence.

Born from a passion for sport, sustainability, and community, Xavathon invites runners of all fitness levels to lace up and make a statement - not just on the track, but for the planet. With a theme of "Towards Greener Footprints," every kilometre run is a stride towards a more conscious future.

From the enthusiasm of a first-time Fun Runner to the determination of a seasoned competitive athlete, Xavathon welcomes everyone. Together we create an electrifying race-day atmosphere in the heart of Kolkata.

Green Initiative

Running for a Greener Future

Every step at Xavathon is a conscious commitment to the environment. We've embedded sustainability into every aspect of the event.

♻

Zero Plastic

Biodegradable cups, reusable bottles, and zero single-use plastic across all race stations.

🌿

Tree Plantation

Every registered runner contributes to a tree plantation drive, creating a lasting green legacy.

🌎

Carbon Offset

We calculate and offset the event's carbon footprint through certified environmental programmes.

🚲

Green Transport

Encouraging participants to use public transport, cycling or carpooling to the venue.

👝

Eco Race Kit

Race kits made from sustainable, recycled materials - fashion meets responsibility.

💧

Water Conservation

Smart hydration stations designed to minimise water wastage throughout the race route.
